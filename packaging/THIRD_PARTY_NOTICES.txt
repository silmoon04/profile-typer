Profile Typer bundles the following dependencies in the Windows app.
The application's MIT license does not replace these dependencies' licenses.

Python 3.12: Python Software Foundation license and notices, included in licenses/Python.txt.
Source: https://github.com/python/cpython/tree/v3.12.7

Qt, PySide6, and Shiboken6 6.9.3: the open source distribution is used under LGPL v3.
The LGPL and referenced GPL terms are included in licenses. Qt and the bindings are
shared libraries in _internal and can be replaced with compatible builds.
Source and build instructions:
https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/qtbase.git/tree/?h=v6.9.3
https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/tree/?h=v6.9.3
https://doc.qt.io/qtforpython-6/building_from_source/index.html
Qt third-party notices: https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qtcore-attribution.html
https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qtgui-attribution.html

PyInstaller: GPL with the bootloader exception. Its license files are copied from
the build environment into licenses/PyInstaller.
Source: https://github.com/pyinstaller/pyinstaller

notices are included beside the fonts in _internal/profile_typer/assets/fonts.
Sources: https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk
https://github.com/googlefonts/spacemono
